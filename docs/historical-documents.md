# Historical document index

Files named `waar-micro-combat-*.md` preserve contracts, relays, handoffs, and
reviews from the original Waar v3 repository. Commands and paths inside them are
historical evidence and may refer to `packages/waar-micro-combat/` or
`var/waar-micro-combat/` in that host.

Links to extracted files point to their current location. References to reports,
captures, or host documents that were not extracted are retained as explicitly
labelled historical paths, not clickable local links. Historical statements
record the state at their delivery date and may have been superseded.

For current executable commands, use the root [README](../README.md). The canonical PO
objective is [the 32-zone objective document](../experiments/objectives/20260909-po-design-01-canonical/acceptance-zones.json).
The [T34 handoff](waar-micro-combat-t34-handoff.md) records the open R1 reservation
from the latest review.
No historical review is converted into a new approval by this extraction.
